PUBLIC WORKS DEPARTMENT — CIVIL DIVISION
PROCUREMENT REQUIREMENT — ORDINARY PORTLAND CEMENT

Department: State Public Works Department
Procurement item: Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC) for RCC building works
Quantity: 5000 bags (50 kg each)

Technical requirement:
1. The cement shall be Ordinary Portland Cement, OPC 43 grade, suitable for general RCC construction.
2. The cement shall meet the required 28-day, 7-day and 3-day compressive strength values.
3. Fineness, soundness and setting-time properties shall conform to the applicable Indian Standard.
4. The cement shall be manufactured by grinding clinker with gypsum and permitted performance improvers.
5. Bags shall be marked with manufacturing date, grade and batch number.
6. The supplier shall also quote for high-strength deformed reinforcement steel bars for the RCC work and plywood for concrete shuttering, to be procured together with the cement.
7. The supplier shall provide valid BIS/ISI mark certification for the supplied cement.

Evaluation:
The procurement team will review the recommended Indian Standards for cement, reinforcement steel and shuttering material, along with certification requirements, before finalizing the tender.
