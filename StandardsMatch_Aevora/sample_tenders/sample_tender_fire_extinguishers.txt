GOVERNMENT DISTRICT OFFICE
PROCUREMENT REQUIREMENT — PORTABLE FIRE EXTINGUISHERS

Department: District Administrative Office
Procurement item: Portable ABC dry-powder fire extinguishers
Application: Protection of occupied government office buildings, electrical rooms and common areas
Quantity: 40 units

Technical requirement:
1. The supplied extinguishers shall be portable and suitable for first-response fire protection.
2. Extinguishing media shall be ABC dry powder.
3. Nominal charge capacity shall be 4 kg per unit.
4. The units shall be suitable for Class A, Class B and Class C fire risks.
5. Cylinder construction shall provide adequate resistance to corrosion and normal handling impacts.
6. The extinguisher shall have safe operating pressure and reliable discharge performance.
7. The product shall include clear operating instructions, identification/marking and tamper-resistant safety arrangements.
8. The supplier shall provide evidence of product performance and construction testing, including relevant pressure, leakage, corrosion and performance checks.
9. The supplied equipment shall support inspection, periodic maintenance and servicing after installation.
10. The supplier shall provide guidance for appropriate selection, installation and placement in occupied office areas.
11. The procurement specification should identify applicable Indian Standards and BIS certification requirements. The officer should not have to know the IS number in advance.

Evaluation:
The procurement team will review the recommended Indian Standards, supporting evidence, related installation/maintenance standards and certification information before finalizing the tender.
