MUNICIPAL CORPORATION — ELECTRICAL DEPARTMENT
PROCUREMENT REQUIREMENT — LED STREET LIGHTING

Department: Municipal Corporation Electrical Wing
Procurement item: Self-ballasted LED lamps and LED street-light luminaires
Quantity: 800 LED lamps + 800 luminaire fixtures

Technical requirement:
1. LED lamps shall be self-ballasted, suitable for general lighting service, rated for 90-110% of supply voltage.
2. Lamps shall meet safety requirements and performance requirements including lumen output, energy efficiency and BEE star rating.
3. Luminaires shall meet general requirements and tests for outdoor street-light fixtures, including ingress protection and thermal performance.
4. All LED lamps and luminaires shall carry valid BIS Compulsory Registration Order (CRO) certification for electronics products.
5. Manufacturer shall provide test reports for photobiological safety and electromagnetic compatibility.
6. The tender should also cover the wiring cable and circuit-breaker requirements for the associated electrical installation.

Evaluation:
The procurement team will review the recommended Indian Standards for LED lamp safety, LED lamp performance, luminaires and related electrical-installation standards, along with mandatory certification requirements, before finalizing the tender.
