MUNICIPAL CORPORATION – PROCUREMENT DIVISION
TENDER NOTICE: SUPPLY OF SAFETY AND ELECTRICAL MATERIALS FOR NEW COMMUNITY HOSPITAL BUILDING

Department: Municipal Corporation, Engineering Wing
Procurement item: Fire safety, electrical, lighting and construction materials
Quantity: 200 nos fire extinguishers, 5000 bags cement, 1500 nos LED lamps

Technical requirements:

1. Fire safety
   Supply of 4 kg ABC dry powder portable fire extinguishers for wards, corridors and offices.
   Extinguishers shall conform to IS 15683:2006 and must pass pressure, corrosion, leakage and
   vibration tests. The supplier shall also provide wheeled fire extinguishers for the generator room.
   Selection, installation, inspection and maintenance schedule shall be included in the offer.

2. Construction materials
   Ordinary Portland Cement OPC 43 grade for RCC building works, meeting compressive strength,
   fineness, soundness and setting time requirements. The supplier shall also quote for deformed
   TMT reinforcement steel bars and structural steel E250 plates for the hospital frame.

3. Electrical and lighting
   9 W self-ballasted LED lamps and LED luminaire light fittings for general lighting, with
   lumen output and safety requirements as per applicable standards.
   Electrical wiring cable 1100 V with PVC insulation, and plug and socket outlets rated 16 A 250 V.

4. Worker safety
   Industrial safety helmets for construction workers with impact and penetration resistance,
   and safety footwear with toe protection and perforation resistance.

5. Water supply
   UPVC pipes for potable water supply and rotational moulded overhead water storage tanks.

Certification:
All supplied products shall carry a valid BIS / ISI mark certification. Test reports and
manufacturer certificates must be submitted along with the bid.

Evaluation:
The procurement team will review the recommended Indian Standards for each item, along with
certification requirements, before finalizing the tender.

Note: Demonstration input for the StandardsMatch SIH26108 prototype. Final applicability and
certification decisions must be verified against current official BIS information.
