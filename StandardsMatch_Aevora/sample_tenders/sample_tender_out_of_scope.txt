STATE DEPARTMENT OF HIGHER EDUCATION - IT PROCUREMENT
TENDER NOTICE: SUPPLY OF COMPUTERS AND SOFTWARE FOR COLLEGE COMPUTER LABS

Procurement item: Laptops, laser printers, software subscriptions and training sessions
Quantity: 50 laptops, 10 laser printers, 100 software subscriptions

Requirements:

1. Laptops
   Supply of 50 laptops with 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD storage, 14-inch full HD display,
   backlit keyboard, webcam and a 3-year on-site warranty. Each laptop shall come with
   a genuine operating system licence and an antivirus subscription for 3 years.

2. Printers
   10 monochrome laser printers with duplex printing, network connectivity and one
   spare toner refill each.

3. Software
   100 annual subscriptions of productivity software, plus 20 licences of a statistics
   and data analysis package for the mathematics department.

4. Training
   Two half-day training sessions for lab assistants on using the new systems.

Commercial terms:
Delivery within 30 days of the purchase order. Payment within 30 days of invoice.
Bidders must submit company registration details, tax documents and references
from two previous customers.

Evaluation:
The purchase committee will compare price, warranty period and delivery time.
