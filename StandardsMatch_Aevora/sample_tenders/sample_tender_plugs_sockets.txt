GOVERNMENT HOSTEL ELECTRICAL PROCUREMENT
PROCUREMENT REQUIREMENT — PLUGS AND SOCKET-OUTLETS

Supply household and similar-purpose plugs and socket-outlets rated up to 250 V and 16 A for hostel rooms and common areas. The supplier shall provide products conforming to the applicable Indian Standard, with testing and BIS certification/conformity evidence. The officer should receive the applicable IS reference from the requirement rather than knowing the standard number in advance.
