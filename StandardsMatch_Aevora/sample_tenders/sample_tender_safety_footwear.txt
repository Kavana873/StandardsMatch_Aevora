STATE PUBLIC WORKS PROCUREMENT
PROCUREMENT REQUIREMENT — SAFETY FOOTWEAR

Supply 250 pairs of protective safety footwear for field and maintenance personnel. Footwear shall include protective toe protection, resistance to perforation, slip resistance and suitable electrical properties for the intended work environment. The specification shall include marking, testing evidence, water resistance and applicable BIS certification requirements. The procurement officer needs the applicable Indian Standard identified from the technical requirement without manually searching for an IS number.
